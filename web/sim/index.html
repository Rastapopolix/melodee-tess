Melodee (tess) Simulator

The melodee-tess firmware for the M-Vave FM-1, running in your browser: the same synth engines, sequencer and Tessera generators as on the device, compiled from the same source.

Getting started

  • Play the demo switches on a Tessera generator on each track and starts the sequencer.
  • PLAY starts and stops. ALGORITHM picks the track, PRESETS its sound.
  • Press SEQ, then turn SELECT to the PATTERN page: KNOB 1 picks the generator (Turing, Euclid, Markov, Morph). EVOLVE, FORM and TWEAK are the next pages.
  • The keys play the selected track. OCT- and OCT+ shift the octave.

Controls

  • Knobs: drag up or down, scroll, or focus one and use the arrow keys.
  • Buttons and keys respond to press and release, so holding works as on the device.
  • Computer keyboard: A to ' play C to F, with W E T Y U O P as the black keys. Space is PLAY.

Not simulated

Saving: projects and presets are lost when you reload. USB and the battery. The CPU limit: your computer is much faster than the FM-1, so something that plays smoothly here can still overload the device.
